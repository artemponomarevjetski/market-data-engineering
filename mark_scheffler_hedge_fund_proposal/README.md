# Hedge-fund proposal presented to Mark Scheffler

Art Ponomarev recalls presenting his hedge-fund proposal to [Mark Scheffler](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mark-scheffler-1433008/). This folder records that part of Art's investment-research history.

## Professional background

Mark Scheffler founded **The Appleton Group**, an independent registered investment adviser in Appleton, Wisconsin, in 2002. [Johnson Financial Group's acquisition announcement](https://www.johnsonfinancialgroup.com/about-us/newsroom/johnson-financial-group-expanding-with-acquisition-in-appleton/) identifies Scheffler as the founder and describes the firm's investment advisory business.

[Johnson Financial Group's 2023 annual report](https://www.johnsonfinancialgroup.com/annual-report/2023-annual-report/solid-fundamentals/) confirms that The Appleton Group joined the firm in **September 2023**, with approximately **$210 million in assets under management** at the time.

- **5 March 2025:** Art reintroduced himself, recalled their earlier investment-strategy discussions, and shared an update on his professional background.
- **15 March (year not displayed in the supplied excerpt):** Mark offered to look at Art's résumé if Art wished, while explaining that his focus had shifted toward a congressional campaign and that he had been away from hiring for some time.

The January 2012 reference suggests an office visit around **2010**. The exact date of the hedge-fund proposal presentation remains unrecorded. The later exchange documents continued professional contact and Mark's willingness to review Art's résumé.
